Designing & Delivering the Greengrass Lite BLE Gateway Workshop

I designed this hands-on workshop to empower engineers and developers to build secure, scalable BLE-to-AWS IoT gateways using the STM32MP135F-DK and AWS IoT Greengrass Lite. My goal was to make advanced edge-to-cloud IoT concepts accessible, actionable, and inspiring for both beginners and experienced professionals.

Featured Videos:

The workshop features a step-by-step guide, custom hardware/software integration, and practical demonstrations. I architected the solution to highlight:
  • Seamless BLE sensor data collection and cloud streaming
  • Robust security and device management using AWS IoT Greengrass Lite
  • Efficient OTA updates and lifecycle management
  • Scalable, low-power gateway design for industrial IoT
To maximize impact, I produced an accompanying video walkthrough that visually demonstrates the setup, deployment, and real-world data streaming. The video showcases my ability to communicate complex technical concepts clearly and effectively, ensuring participants can follow along and replicate the solution.

  • Empowering others to innovate with modern cloud and embedded technologies
I am passionate about sharing knowledge and enabling others to succeed in the rapidly evolving IoT landscape. This project is a testament to my ability to design, build, and deliver impactful technical workshops.
